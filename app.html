Haggle Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page runs haggle arithmetic over your own prices: the gap between the ask and the offer, where the offer sits against your floor, the split-the-difference counter rounded to your step, and the halving ladder toward the offer. It does not know what the item is, what it is worth, or whether any price is fair, and it gives no verdict that you should take or refuse a deal. Every number here is your own entry.

The prices

The asking price and the offer on the table, as dollar amounts like 45 or 45.00.

The guardrails

Your walk-away floor, and the step the counter rounds to: one of 0.01, 0.05, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50 or 1.00.

The gap truth

Gap: the distance between the ask and the offer, in dollars and as a share of the ask.

The floor truth

Floor: where the offer sits against your own walk-away line.

The middle truth

Middle: one split of the difference, rounded to your step.

The ladder truth

Ladder: where the price lands if you keep halving the remaining gap.

Haggle Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
