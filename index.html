Haggle Math

The gap between the ask and the offer, and exactly where a counter lands.

Boundary: arithmetic only Haggle Math does arithmetic over prices you enter. It does not know what the item is worth, whether a price is fair, or whether you should take a deal, and it gives no verdict on the deal. Every number is your own entry.

The problem

A buyer names one number, you named another, and now both of you are doing mental arithmetic under social pressure: how far apart are we, is this under my floor, what do I counter with? Most counters are picked by feel and end in odd cents nobody can split twice.

What it tells you

Enter the asking price, the offer, your walk-away floor, and the step you want counters rounded to. Haggle Math answers four truths with exact cent arithmetic: the gap in dollars and as a share of the ask, where the offer sits against your floor, the split-the-difference counter rounded to your step, and the halving ladder that shows where the price lands after one, two and three more splits.

The honesty

An offer under your floor says so in plain words, with the exact dollars it misses by. And when the gap is smaller than your rounding step, the page says the step swallows it instead of inventing a counter it cannot express.
